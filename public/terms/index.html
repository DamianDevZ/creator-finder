# Terms of Service — Creator Finder

Creator Finder is a tool for discovering YouTube creators who cover games
similar to a given Steam title, intended to help developers with marketing
and outreach.

## Usage

- The service provides read-only access to public YouTube and Steam data via
  their official APIs.
- Results are informational estimates (subscriber counts, view averages,
  detected languages, contact details found in public descriptions) and are
  provided "as is" without warranty of accuracy or completeness.
- You are responsible for how you use the contact information surfaced by the
  tool. Outreach emails should comply with applicable laws (e.g. CAN-SPAM,
  GDPR) and YouTube's own Terms of Service.

## API compliance

- YouTube API data is displayed as returned by the API, cached temporarily to
  reduce quota usage, and is not redistributed in bulk.
- No YouTube content is downloaded, re-hosted, or modified.

## Liability

The tool is provided without guarantees of uptime or results. Use at your own
discretion.
